
## Die viewer: two-finger scroll no longer stalls over overlays
- Wheel listener in `frontend/src/renderer/useCanvasGestures.ts` now bound on the canvas parent; handles wheels on the canvas or on elements inside `[data-canvas-wheel]`.
- Tagged: floorplan region SVGs (FloorplanOverlay), FloorplanEditHandles svg, comment pins (CommentOverlay). Hover cursors unchanged; panels/popovers keep native scroll.
- New overlays that capture pointer events should add `data-canvas-wheel`.
